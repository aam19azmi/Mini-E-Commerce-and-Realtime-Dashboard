1. Product Requirement Document (PRD)

1.1 Executive Summary & Mission

NovaStore is an enterprise-grade digital commerce platform engineered for single-merchant operations. It eliminates shopping barriers through a frictionless multi-product guest checkout while equipping store administrators and warehouse operators with a real-time command center powered by Supabase PostgreSQL WebSockets.

1.2 Core Specifications & Functional Modules

  • Frictionless Guest Checkout (Multi-Product): Customers can browse items, adjust quantities, add multiple different products to the cart, and complete orders with zero registration or login requirements.
  • Interactive Leaflet Map & Pinning: Full direct click-to-pin, draggable marker, GPS auto-detect, and built-in offline search geocoding across Indonesian and global cities (Jakarta, Bandung, Bali, Medan, New York, Delhi, Shanghai, etc.).
  • Dynamic 4-Tier Logistics Classifier: Intelligent transit mode classification:
    • Local Intra-City (< 35 km): 🛵 Same-Day Motorbike (GoSend/Grab) + Domestic Couriers.
    • Overland Regional (35 – 750 km): 🚚 Ground Fleet Vans & Trucks (JNE REG, J&T EZ, SPX Eco, SiCepat BEST).
    • Inter-Island Nationwide (750 – 3,800 km): ✈️ Commercial Air Cargo Planes & Express Maritime RORO Ships.
    • International Cross-Border (> 3,800 km): 🌐 Global Air Freight Carriers (DHL Express Worldwide, FedEx International, Pos Indonesia EMS, UPS Worldwide).
  • 2.5% Administrative Service Fee: Automatically calculated as Math.round(cartTotal * 0.025) and itemized transparently across checkout, confirmation receipts, and official corporate invoices.
  • Kepabeanan (Bea Cukai) & Tax Compliance: Itemizes domestic PPN 11% tax, Indonesian 0% Export Duty exemption (*Bebas Bea Keluar*), 0% Zero-Rated VAT Export, and destination country DDU (*Delivered Duty Unpaid*) clearance.
  • Zero-Fee Direct Payment Verification: Direct 0% MDR Instant QRIS and Manual Bank Transfer (BCA, Mandiri) with payment receipt screenshot upload and 1-click admin approval/rejection.
  • Real-time Operations Dashboard: Real-time WebSocket subscriptions on PostgreSQL changes, visual KPI analytics graphs (Recharts), inventory CRUD, and 1-click CSV/Excel export.
  • Storefront Corporate Footer & Store Location: Rich footer featuring official headquarters location in Kuningan, Jakarta Selatan, customer service channels (+62 812-9876-5432 / support@novastore.com), operating hours, official logistics & payment partner badges.
  • Customer Return & Refund (RMA) SOP: Formalized retail guarantee where the customer ships the item back to the warehouse first → Physical inspection → Admin changes status to Cancelled (inventory stock is automatically restored) → 100% monetary refund issued within 24 hours.
  • Official Corporate Tax Invoices: Dedicated /invoice/[id] route formatted with DPP (Nilai Kena Pajak), PPN 11% / PPN Ekspor 0%, courier delivery line item, administrative fees, and verified scannable QR verification code.

1.3 Mathematical & Fee Formulations

• Product Subtotal = Sum(Unit Price × Quantity)
• Shipping Tariff = Base Cost + (Distance in KM × Tariff per KM)
• Admin Fee (Biaya Layanan 2.5%) = Round(Product Subtotal × 0.025)
• Grand Total = Product Subtotal + Shipping Tariff + Admin Fee
• Domestic Tax Base (DPP) = Round(Product Subtotal / 1.11)
• Domestic PPN 11% = Product Subtotal - DPP
• International PPN Ekspor = Rp 0 (0% Zero-Rated Export)
• Indonesian Bea Keluar = Rp 0 (0% Free Export Tariff)

2. Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) & Database Schema

The database is architected on Supabase PostgreSQL with performance indexes and Row Level Security (RLS):

  • products Table: id (UUID, PK), name (VARCHAR), description (TEXT), price (NUMERIC), stock (INTEGER), image_url (TEXT), category (VARCHAR), created_at (TIMESTAMPTZ).
  • orders Table: id (UUID, PK), customer_name, customer_email, customer_phone, customer_address, total_amount, admin_fee, shipping_courier, shipping_cost, destination_lat, destination_lng, payment_proof_url, payment_verified, status, payment_method, created_at.
  • order_items Table: id (UUID, PK), order_id (FK -> orders), product_id (FK -> products), quantity, unit_price, subtotal, created_at.

3. System Architecture & Flowchart (Alur Pikir)

3.1 Step-by-Step System Flow (Alur Pikir)

  1. Customer Catalog & Multi-Product Cart: Customer browses catalog, specifies quantities, and adds items to the slide-over cart drawer.
  2. Map Location & Courier Selection: Customer opens checkout, pins GPS coordinates on the interactive map, and chooses their preferred courier.
  3. Fee Calculation & Payment Upload: System calculates the 2.5% administrative fee. Customer scans QRIS or transfers to bank accounts, attaching a payment receipt screenshot.
  4. Order Broadcast: Order is written to Supabase PostgreSQL, instantly triggering a real-time WebSocket push alert to the Admin Dashboard without page refresh.
  5. Admin Verification: Admin inspects the payment proof screenshot in full-resolution lightbox and views the itemized products breakdown (product thumbnails, SKUs, names, quantities, and subtotals) before 1-click approving the transaction.
  6. Fulfillment & Itemized Invoice: Order status transitions from Pending → Processing → Completed. Scannable corporate tax invoices with dynamic itemized product tables, digital QR verification codes, and cashier officer signatures are generated for customer and accounting verification.
